Detailed Assembly Instructions

Step 10.1: Load and Secure Chair Frame Blank

مرحله ۱۰.۱: بارگیری و محکم کردن قطعه خام بدنه صندلی

Setup: Ensure the correct fixture (Fixture ID: FIX-CHAIR-FRAME-01) is installed and calibrated.

Operation:

  1. Place the wooden blank onto the fixture, aligning it with the locating pins.
  2. Activate the vacuum clamp and verify it is holding securely.
  3. Close the safety door and start program "CHAIR-FRAME-V2".
  4. Monitor the cutting process for any abnormal sounds or vibrations.

Quality Check: Visually inspect the tenon joints after machining. They should be smooth and fit snugly without excessive force.

Step 10.2: Machine Armrest Mounting Holes

مرحله ۱۰.۲: ماشین‌کاری سوراخ‌های اتصال دسته‌ها

Setup: Switch to the armrest drilling program. Verify drill bit size (8mm) and sharpness.

Operation:

  1. Load the frame into the second fixture.
  2. Run program "CHAIR-ARMREST-DRILL".
  3. Ensure the holes are drilled perpendicular to the frame surface.

Quality Check: Check hole diameter with a go/no-go gauge. Check for any tear-out on the wood surface.

Step 20.1: Assemble Chair Frame and Armrests

مرحله ۲۰.۱: مونتاژ بدنه صندلی و دسته‌ها

Setup: Gather all components: 1x Frame, 2x Armrests, 4x M6x50mm bolts, 4x M6 washers.

Operation:

  1. Attach each armrest to the frame using two M6 bolts and washers.
  2. Hand-tighten the bolts first, then use a torque wrench to tighten to 8 Nm.
  3. Verify the armrests are level and symmetrical.

Quality Check: Check that the armrests are firmly attached and do not wobble. Visually inspect for any scratches during assembly.

Step 20.2: Install Seat and Backrest Cushions

مرحله ۲۰.۲: نصب کوسن نشیمن و پشتی

Setup: Ensure the upholstery is clean and free of defects. Prepare the adhesive spray gun.

Operation:

  1. Apply a thin, even layer of industrial adhesive to the seat pan.
  2. Carefully align and press the seat cushion onto the pan.
  3. Repeat the process for the backrest cushion.
  4. Allow the adhesive to cure for 30 minutes before handling.

Quality Check: Check for proper alignment. Ensure there are no wrinkles or loose edges on the fabric. Verify the cushion is firmly attached.

Step 20.3: Final Inspection and Packaging

مرحله ۲۰.۳: بازرسی نهایی و بسته‌بندی

Setup: Prepare the final inspection checklist and packaging materials (box, foam, plastic wrap).

Operation:

  1. Perform a final visual inspection of the entire chair: check for scratches, loose threads, or misaligned parts.
  2. Test the height adjustment mechanism and swivel function.
  3. Wrap the chair in protective foam and plastic.
  4. Place it in the designated box and seal it.

Quality Check: Verify the product matches the order specification. Check that the serial number label is correctly applied.

Safety Notes

⚠️ Important Safety Warnings

  • CNC Machining: Always wear safety glasses and hearing protection. Ensure the safety door is closed before starting any program. Do not reach into the machine while it is running.
  • Assembly & Upholstery: Use gloves to prevent splinters and cuts. Adhesive is flammable and harmful if inhaled; use in a well-ventilated area and wear a respirator.
  • General: Keep the work area clean and free of debris. Use proper lifting techniques when moving heavy components.
